I lead the Gemini Post-Training effort at Google DeepMind in Mountain View, where my team works on reinforcement learning, reasoning, alignment, evaluation, and unified modeling recipes across all the Gemini models.

I joined Google in 2015 after completing my M.S. in Computer Science at Stanford University, where I worked with Christopher Manning in the Stanford NLP Group. Earlier at Google Research, I worked on Google's Neural Machine Translation (GNMT) system, pioneered single-model zero-shot multilingual translation across 100+ languages in Google Translate, co-created the XTREME cross-lingual benchmark suite, and researched direct speech-to-speech translation and joint speech-text pre-training.
